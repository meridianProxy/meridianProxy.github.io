The network

Addresses that look like what they are: ordinary people at home.

A datacentre IP is a datacentre IP, and most sites can tell within a request or two. Meridian exits from consumer connections on real ISPs, so a request looks like it came from a household - because it did.

It rotates unless you say otherwise

Out of the box, a plain login hands you a different address on every connection. That is the right default for anything that crawls, checks or collects at volume.

Or hold one address

Add a session token and the same exit stays with you for around ten minutes. Long enough to log in, work through a flow, and look like one continuous visitor.

Countries, honestly

You can prefer a country and take whatever is free, or demand one and be told no when nothing is available. We do not claim city or ZIP targeting, because we do not do it.

SOCKS5 and HTTPS

Both on the same credentials, so you do not juggle two accounts. Works with Playwright, Puppeteer, Selenium, requests, Go, or plain curl without a plugin.

Rules that stack

A country, a session and a rotation mode are all just words in the username, and they chain. One string covers every combination you will ever need.

You can see the meter

Ask the bot for your balance whenever you like, and it pings you at eighty percent so a long job never dies halfway through for a reason you did not see coming.

Questions

Worth knowing before you pay.

Anything not covered here, ask the bot - you will get a person, not a form.

What actually is a residential proxy?

Instead of your request arriving from a server in a datacentre, it arrives from an ordinary household connection. The destination sees a normal consumer IP on a normal ISP, which is why it is treated differently from a hosting range that gets challenged on sight.

How do I get an account?

Message the bot and describe what you are running. You will get payment details, and once that clears you receive a single-use code. Redeem the code in the same chat and your endpoint and login are issued on the spot.

Do you charge monthly?

No. There is no subscription, no seat and no recurring charge of any kind. You buy gigabytes when you want them and spend them whenever you like. If you do not touch the network for six months, nothing bills you and nothing expires.

What is the smallest amount I can buy?

$20, which is a few gigabytes at our rate. It exists so you can try the network against your real targets before committing to anything substantial.

Can I pick a city or a carrier?

No - targeting is country level. You can prefer a country and accept a fallback, or require one and get a refusal when none is free, but we do not offer city, ZIP, carrier or ASN filters. We would rather say so plainly than list filters that quietly do nothing.

How do I keep the same IP?

Add a session token to the username and one exit stays with you for around ten minutes, long enough to complete a login or work through a multi-step flow. Leave it off and you get a fresh address on every connection.

How do I keep track of what I have used?

Ask the bot for your balance at any time and it will show what you have spent against your cap. It also messages you automatically at about eighty percent, so you are never cut off in the middle of a run without warning.

Is this page different from the main site?

It is the same design, the same copy and the same components. Two things are different: the hero background is drawn in CSS rather than played from a CDN, and the typeface is served from this address instead of a font host. The only visible omission is the one-second intro animation, which needs scripting to start.

Is the Telegram bot safe to reach from here?

Telegram is not a Tor service, so contacting the bot takes you out of the Tor network and onto ordinary TLS. That is a decision worth making deliberately rather than by accident. An onion address for contact is coming; until then, use a dedicated Telegram account if that matters to you.
