Terms of Service

These terms govern your use of the Meridian residential proxy network ("the Service"). By purchasing, redeeming or using access, you accept them.

1. The Service

Meridian provides proxy access to a rotating pool of residential network exits. Traffic is measured in bytes and billed per gigabyte against a prepaid balance. Endpoints, protocols and credentials are issued per account.

2. Accounts and access

  • Accounts are issued to a single operator and are not transferable.
  • You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential.
  • Credentials may be rotated on request if you believe they have been exposed.
  • Sharing, reselling or sub-licensing access without written agreement is prohibited.

3. Balances and billing

  • All access is prepaid. Service is available only while a positive balance remains.
  • When a balance is exhausted, connections are refused until the account is topped up.
  • Balances do not expire.
  • Billing is based on bytes transferred, measured on our infrastructure while the connection is active. Usage records are retained for accounting and abuse prevention.

4. Availability

The pool consists of real residential connections that join and leave the network continuously, so exit availability, country coverage and exit identity vary over time. Meridian does not guarantee a specific exit, a specific country, or uninterrupted availability. Requests that cannot be satisfied under the targeting rules you specified are refused rather than silently routed elsewhere, unless you selected a preferred-country rule.

5. Acceptable use

Your use of the Service is subject to our Acceptable Use Policy below, which forms part of these terms. Accounts found in breach may be suspended or terminated.

6. Termination

You may stop using the Service at any time. We may suspend or terminate an account that breaches these terms or the Acceptable Use Policy, that presents a risk to network integrity or to third parties, or that is the subject of a legitimate complaint. Termination for breach does not entitle you to a refund of any remaining balance.

7. No warranty

The Service is provided "as is" and "as available", without warranties of any kind, express or implied, including fitness for a particular purpose. You are responsible for determining whether the Service is suitable for your workload and compliant with the rules that apply to you.

8. Limitation of liability

To the maximum extent permitted by law, Meridian's total liability arising from or relating to the Service is limited to the amount you paid for the Service in the three months preceding the claim. We are not liable for indirect, incidental or consequential losses, including lost profits, lost data or business interruption.

9. Changes

These terms may be updated from time to time. Material changes will be communicated through the account channel you use to access the Service. Continued use after a change constitutes acceptance.

Privacy Policy

This policy explains what we record when you use the Service, why, and for how long. It is deliberately short, because we collect as little as a working proxy network can.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — the identifier you choose, a salted hash of your password, your balance and your usage counters.
  • Connection metadata — timestamps, bytes transferred in each direction, and the exit assigned to a connection. Billing and abuse prevention run on this.
  • Service messages — if you contact us through our messaging channel, that conversation exists there as well as with us.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not inspect, store or sell the content of the traffic you send through the network.
  • We do not build advertising or marketing profiles.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

3. Why we keep it

  • To meter usage and bill accurately against your prepaid balance.
  • To detect and stop abuse that would harm the network or third parties.
  • To answer support requests about your own account.

4. Retention

Usage and billing records are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, so historical balances and disputes remain auditable. Connection metadata is kept only as long as it is needed for accounting and abuse prevention.

5. Sharing

We do not disclose account data to third parties except where we are legally required to, or where disclosure is necessary to investigate abuse that threatens the network or other parties.

6. Your choices

  • You can request rotation of your credentials at any time.
  • You can close your account by contacting us; usage history is removed on a rolling basis afterwards.
  • Do not send us sensitive personal information — we do not need it to provide the Service.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to administrative systems is restricted and authenticated. No system is perfectly secure, so treat your proxy credentials as sensitive and rotate them if you suspect exposure.

Refund Policy

Access is prepaid and metered. This policy explains what that means in practice before you spend anything.

1. Before you buy

The minimum purchase is $20 — several gigabytes at our rates. It exists so you can test the network against your real targets before committing to real volume. We do not offer free trials, and we do not treat the minimum as a trial: it is normal service you keep and spend.

2. Balances

  • Balances do not expire. Buy now, use the traffic whenever you need it.
  • Balances are held against your account identifier and are not transferable.

3. Traffic already consumed

Traffic that has already passed through the network cannot be refunded. Once gigabytes are delivered, the cost of serving them has been incurred and cannot be recovered.

4. Unused balance

Unused balance may be refunded at our discretion on request, less any payment-processing fees incurred on the original transaction. Refunds are issued to the same method used for payment where technically possible.

5. Service problems

If you experience a sustained failure on our side, contact us before requesting a refund. In most cases the fastest resolution is a credit to your balance rather than a reversal.

6. No refund in these cases

  • Accounts terminated for breaching the Acceptable Use Policy.
  • Unsuitable results caused by your own integration, targeting choices or tooling.
  • Country availability on the preferred-country tier, which falls back by design rather than failing.
  • Traffic consumed by a workload you chose to run.

7. Chargebacks

Raising a payment reversal without contacting us first results in immediate suspension of the account while the dispute is open. If you have a problem with a payment, message us — it is faster for both of us.

Acceptable Use Policy

This policy defines the boundary of what Meridian access may be used for. It applies to every account and forms part of our Terms of Service. Accounts that breach it are suspended, and balances on terminated accounts are not refunded.

1. Intended use

Meridian is built for lawful data collection and automation: price and catalogue monitoring, market research, SEO and rank tracking, ad verification, brand protection, and QA or load testing of systems you own — workloads that need geographically diverse residential viewpoints.

2. Prohibited use

You must not use the Service, directly or indirectly, for:

  • Spam, unsolicited bulk messaging, or mass account creation.
  • Fraud, financial crime, or purchasing goods or services with fraudulent intent.
  • Testing or using stolen payment credentials.
  • Credential stuffing, brute-force authentication, or unauthorised access to any system.
  • Distributing malware, ransomware, or any malicious code.
  • Any content or activity involving the sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Harassment, stalking, threats, or targeted abuse of any person.
  • Denial-of-service activity, network scanning, or attacks against infrastructure you do not own or have written permission to test.
  • Evading sanctions, or any activity prohibited under the laws applicable to you or to us.
  • Reselling, sub-licensing or sharing access without written agreement.

3. Network abuse

  • Do not open excessive concurrent connections to a single destination to the point where it degrades that destination's service or network stability.
  • Do not send traffic designed to overwhelm, probe or fingerprint infrastructure you do not own.
  • Do not attempt to interfere with, reverse engineer or overload the network itself.

4. Enforcement

  • We may suspend an account immediately, without notice, where a breach is occurring or imminent.
  • Suspension freezes access: connections are refused until the matter is resolved.
  • Where a breach is clear and material, the account is terminated and remaining balance is forfeited.
  • Where a complaint is credible but disputed, we will tell you what we observed and give you the opportunity to explain before deciding.

5. Your responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring your activity is lawful where you operate, that you are entitled to collect the data you collect, and that you comply with the terms of the sites you access. We do not pre-approve individual workloads.
