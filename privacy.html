Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This policy explains what Meridian records when you use the Service, why we record it, and how long we keep it. It is deliberately short, because we collect as little as a working proxy network can.

1. What we collect

  • Account data — the identifier you choose, a salted hash of your password, your balance and your usage counters.
  • Connection metadata — timestamps, bytes transferred in each direction, and the exit assigned to a connection. This is what billing and abuse prevention run on.
  • Service messages — if you contact us through our Telegram channel, that conversation exists in Telegram's systems as well as ours.

2. What we do not do

  • We do not inspect, store or sell the content of the traffic you send through the network.
  • We do not build advertising or marketing profiles.
  • We do not sell or rent your data to third parties.

3. Why we keep it

  • To meter usage and bill accurately against your prepaid balance.
  • To detect and stop abuse that would harm the network or third parties.
  • To answer support requests about your own account.

4. Retention

Usage and billing records are kept while your account is active and for a reasonable period afterwards, so that historical balances and disputes remain auditable. Connection metadata is kept for as long as it is needed for accounting and abuse prevention, then discarded.

5. Sharing

We do not disclose account data to third parties except where we are legally required to, or where disclosure is necessary to investigate abuse that threatens the network or other parties.

6. Your choices

  • You can request rotation of your credentials at any time.
  • You can close your account by contacting us; usage history is removed on a rolling basis afterwards, except where it must be retained for accounting.
  • You should not send sensitive personal information to us — we do not need it to provide the Service.

7. Security

Passwords are stored only as salted hashes. Access to administrative systems is restricted and authenticated. No system is perfectly secure, and you should treat your proxy credentials as sensitive and rotate them if you suspect exposure.

8. Contact

Privacy questions: reach us on Telegram.
