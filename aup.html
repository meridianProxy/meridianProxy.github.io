Acceptable Use Policy

Last updated: January 2026

This policy defines the boundary of what Meridian access may be used for. It applies to every account and forms part of our Terms of Service. Accounts that breach it are suspended, and balances on terminated accounts are not refunded.

1. Intended use

Meridian is built for lawful data collection and automation: price and catalogue monitoring, market research, SEO and rank tracking, ad verification, brand protection, QA and load testing of your own systems, and similar workloads that need geographically diverse residential viewpoints.

2. Prohibited use

You must not use the Service, directly or indirectly, for:

  • Spam, unsolicited bulk messaging, or mass account creation on any platform.
  • Fraud, financial crime, or the purchase of goods or services with fraudulent intent.
  • Testing or using stolen payment credentials, including "carding".
  • Credential stuffing, brute-force authentication attempts, or unauthorised access to any system.
  • Distributing malware, ransomware, or any malicious code.
  • Any content or activity involving the sexual exploitation of minors.
  • Harassment, stalking, threats, or targeted abuse of any person.
  • Denial-of-service activity, network scanning, or attacks against any infrastructure you do not own or have written permission to test.
  • Evading sanctions, or any activity prohibited under the laws applicable to you or to us.
  • Reselling, sub-licensing or sharing access without written agreement.

3. Network abuse

Regardless of intent, you must not:

  • Open excessive concurrent connections to a single destination, to the point where it degrades that destination's service or the network's stability.
  • Send traffic designed to overwhelm, probe or fingerprint infrastructure you do not own.
  • Attempt to interfere with, reverse engineer or overload the network itself.

4. Enforcement

  • We may suspend an account immediately, without notice, where we believe a breach is occurring or imminent.
  • Suspension freezes access: connections are refused until the matter is resolved.
  • Where a breach is clear and material, the account is terminated and remaining balance is forfeited.
  • Where a complaint is credible but disputed, we will tell you what we observed and give you the opportunity to explain before deciding.

5. Your responsibility

You are responsible for ensuring that your activity is lawful where you operate, that you are entitled to collect the data you are collecting, and that you comply with the terms of the sites you access. We do not pre-approve individual workloads.

6. Reporting

To report abuse of the network, contact us on Telegram. Reports that identify a specific account and pattern of activity are actioned fastest.
